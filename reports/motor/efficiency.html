Study 7 · efficiency

Efficiency map

How efficient is the motor across its torque-speed range, and does it hold its rated 50 kW from base speed to 6000 rpm within the 500 V bus and the 212 A copper limit? The map comes from look-up tables of FEMM solves, searched for the lowest-loss current at every operating point.

Framing (user's decision, 2026-11-22): motor only, copper and iron loss; no mechanical, magnet eddy-current, AC copper or inverter loss, so these efficiencies are upper bounds on what a dynamometer would measure. Two corners of a temperature band: cold (everything at 20 °C) and hot (windings 100 °C, magnets 80 °C). Bus 500 V; design current 212 A rms.

Blind: no ORNL efficiency value has been opened. The sample points are chosen to span the plane, not taken from any ORNL test plan.

Method

  • dq tables. At each d-axis current, one FEMM run per q-axis current over one electrical period at 1000 rpm gives the mean torque, the dq flux linkages and the iron-loss coefficients of P = A f + B f² + C f1.5. One table per magnet temperature.
  • The search. The tables are interpolated (cubic) onto a 1 A grid. At each speed and torque, the feasible current with the least copper plus iron loss is chosen, under the voltage limit Vdc/√6 (204 V phase rms, the resistive drop included) and the current limit.
  • Copper. 72 series turns per phase, 8.49 mm² of bare copper per turn, end connections estimated from the geometry: 59.6 mΩ per phase at 20 °C, 78.4 mΩ at 100 °C.
