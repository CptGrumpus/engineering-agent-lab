Open-circuit electromagnetics

With no current in the windings, the magnets alone set the flux linkage. That gives the back-EMF constant (the blind prediction for ORNL) and answers REQ-M1: if the inverter stops switching at top speed, does the motor's own voltage stay under the 500 V bus?

REQ-M1 fails, about twice over. The line-to-line back-EMF peak at 6000 rpm is about 1000-1030 V against the 500 V bus. That is expected for an interior-magnet design: above base speed the drive depends on field weakening, and a fault at top speed needs protection. The flux-linkage harmonics add 9.5 % to the peak over the fundamental on our machine, and 13 % on Pyleecan's, whose wider slots make the field less sinusoidal.

Method

  • 2D magnetostatic FEMM solves through Pyleecan, at 1000 rpm over one electrical period in 48 steps, using the machine's angular and time periodicity.
  • λ1 is the mean fundamental amplitude of the three phase flux linkages. The back-EMF constant is √3 ωe λ1 / √2 at 1000 rpm, with 4 pole pairs.
  • The peak at 6000 rpm is the largest |eab| from a spectral derivative of φa − φb, harmonics included, scaled by the speed ratio.
  • Mesh fineness divides all of Pyleecan's FEMM mesh sizes (at fineness 1: air gap 0.25 mm, rotor iron 1.94 mm, magnets 3.9 mm, teeth 4.3 mm, yoke 4.8 mm).

Case record: predictions before the run, results after
Study 7, first case: the 2004 Toyota Prius traction motor at open circuit (no current), Pyleecan + FEMM.

Decision this case informs: if the inverter stops switching at top speed (a fault), the magnets alone drive the
phase voltage: the "uncontrolled generator" condition. Does the motor's line-to-line back-EMF at its maximum speed
stay within the 500 V maximum bus voltage, or must the drive rely on active field weakening and protection? It
also fixes the back-EMF constant that the blind ORNL validation will compare against later (BACKLOG, study 7).

Design inputs: Pyleecan's Toyota_Prius.json (master 1e940df). Spot-checked against ORNL/TM-2004/137 (design report):
3.3 in stack exactly; series winding (2004); 200-500 V boosted bus; 400 N m peak torque. Not yet confirmed: the
diameters and air gap (in the report's figures). Assumptions: generic NdFeB magnet (Br 1.24 T at 20 C, recoil
permeability 1.05), M400-50A laminations (0.5 mm; the real ones are 0.36 mm; irrelevant at open circuit except
through the B-H curve).

Prototype (hand runs before this case): flux-linkage fundamental 0.20907 Wb at 48 and at 96 steps per electrical
  period (time resolution settled; 24 steps per mechanical revolution had given only 6 per electrical period);
  default and doubled mesh fineness agree to 1e-4; winding theory from the air-gap field 0.6-0.7 % above FEMM's
  coil integration; cogging torque mean 0.07 N m.

Predictions, written BEFORE the evidence run (and before any ORNL performance data has been opened):
  P1 Every point check passes on every mesh: phases within 1e-3 of each other and 120 deg apart, winding theory
     within 1 %, open-circuit torque under 1e-3 of the rated 400 N m.
  P2 Flux-linkage fundamental 0.2091 Wb, back-EMF 107.25 V line-to-line rms per 1000 rpm, mesh GCI under 0.1 %.
     This is the blind prediction for the ORNL comparison.
  P3 Line-to-line back-EMF peak at 6000 rpm about 910-930 V (the fundamental alone gives 910 V): REQ-M1 FAILS by
     about 80 %. Expected for this interior-PM design: it relies on field weakening above base speed.

Smoke test (scratch, mesh fineness 1, before the evidence run): every check passes (phase symmetry 1.8e-4, phase
  shift 0.004 deg, winding theory 0.54 %, open-circuit torque 2e-5 of rated). lambda1 0.20906 Wb, 107.253 V per
  1000 rpm (P2 held). The line-to-line peak at 6000 rpm is 1027 V, not 910-930: the flux-linkage harmonics add 13 %
  to the peak over the fundamental, more than the few percent assumed. P3 revised to about 1030 V; the verdict
  (REQ-M1 fails) is unchanged.

Results (run 20261121-194454_motor_prius_oc_mesh): every point check passes on every mesh (15/15). Line-to-line peak at
  6000 rpm 1026.7 / 1028.3 / 1028.0 V at mesh fineness 1 / 2 / 4 (oscillatory, uncertainty 0.10 %): REQ-M1 VERIFIED
  FAIL, about twice the 500 V bus. The drive depends on field weakening and fault protection at high speed (P3 as
  revised held). Flux-linkage fundamental 0.20906 / 0.20884 / 0.20864 Wb: monotone but observed order 0.19,
  extrapolated 0.20722; GCI 0.85 % passes the 1 % limit, but such a low order suggests the global refinement barely
  reaches what matters (likely the saturated rotor bridges). P2's GCI under 0.1 % MISSED. Revised blind prediction,
  still before any ORNL performance data is opened: back-EMF 107.0 V rms line-to-line per 1000 rpm at the finest
  mesh, with up to about 1 % attributable to the mesh (about 106 V extrapolated). Next: refine the bridges locally
  and check the order recovers. Solve times 14 / 28 / 101 s.

Scratch sensitivity before this case (BACKLOG, 21:45): the radii alone (air gap 0.775 mm) lowered the back-EMF

drawing's 7.40 mm tooth it is about 1.27 T. Less tooth saturation means less magnet MMF lost in the iron.
