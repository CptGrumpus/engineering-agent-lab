Study 20 · Test dataIn progress Traction Motor: Finding the Missing Flux

Where it standsModelled from the client's data with nothing tuned, the motor reads 17.7 % high on back-EMF and 17 to 19 % high on torque against the laboratory's test, so the model carries about 18 % more magnet flux than the real motor. Every client input matches the parts list. Its own page has the results and the design checks.

Ruled outThe lamination steel in the rotor's bridges: three grades moved the back-EMF by −0.35 to +0.14 %, as my estimate of under 1 % said. End effects, the part of the field a 2D model leaves out: 1.3 to 3.7 %, too small to need a 3D model.

Testing nowA second, independent 2D model in ElmerFEM on the same geometry, to check the field itself. Its predictions were written before the run.

NextThe leading suspect is how fully the magnets are magnetised in the assembled rotor. That's a question for the client, and a test on one magnet would settle it. Then the efficiency map and the power ratings, once the flux is explained.

Study 17 · Fluid-structure interactionIn progress Flexible Flag Behind a Cylinder

Why it's harderTwo-way fluid-structure interaction, where the flow bends the flag and the bent flag changes the flow.

So farEach half reproduced the published Turek-Hron benchmark on its own before the two were coupled. Coupled and steady, drag and lift are verified at 0.03 % and 0.18 % from the published values. In the two cases where the flag swings by itself, every published quantity comes out within 2.1 % and 9 % on one mesh, inside the benchmark's own spread between meshes. Those aren't verified yet: the check on the force passed between the two solvers turned out to be defective, so I've replaced it, and both swinging cases are now running on three meshes each, side by side.

Caught on the wayOne regenerated mesh wrapped a single cell around the flag's tip corner and read the tip deflection 28 % low, with every other check passing. There's now a check on the cells next to the flag, and 24 earlier meshes were scanned with it. Only that one was affected.
