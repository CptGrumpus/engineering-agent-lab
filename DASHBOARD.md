## Where the dashboard lives

https://cptgrumpus.github.io/engineering-agent-lab/, built from `site/` and served from `docs/`. This is the link to give clients. Since 2026-11-22 it's a site of 21 pages: a home page, one page per study (or report of related studies), and pages for how I work, the grading tasks, the studies in progress and the requirements table. Each study has its own address, so a link to one study can go to one client.

To rebuild after a change: run `site/build_v2.py`, then `site/export_docs.py`, then `site/check_viewer3d.py` (3D checks and script errors on every page, in headless Chrome), then commit and push. See [site/README.md](site/README.md) for how the site is put together.
