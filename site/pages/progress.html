Study 17 · Fluid-structure interactionIn progress Flexible flag behind a cylinder

Why it's harderTwo-way fluid-structure interaction, where the flow bends the flag and the bent flag changes the flow.

So farEach half has to reproduce the published Turek-Hron benchmark before the two are coupled. The solid's sag matches to 0.01 %, and it swings at 1.095 Hz against the published 1.096 to 1.100. The fluid's drag matches to 0.07 % and 0.014 % at the two flow speeds. Lift is within 0.7 % but scatters from mesh to mesh, so I'm holding that verdict back. The first coupled case has run: its drag converges to within 0.03 % of the benchmark, and the flag tip lands within 1 %, but the tip still drifts as the mesh is refined and the lift carries noise from the coupling, so it isn't verified yet. A revision is running.
