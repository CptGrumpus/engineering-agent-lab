Study 17 · Fluid-structure interactionIn progress Flexible flag behind a cylinder

Why it's harderTwo-way fluid-structure interaction, where the flow bends the flag and the bent flag changes the flow.

So farEach half has to reproduce the published Turek-Hron benchmark before the two are coupled. The solid's sag matches to 0.015 %, and it swings at 1.095 Hz against the published 1.096 to 1.100. The fluid's drag matches to 0.07 % and 0.014 % at the two flow speeds. The first coupled case, the steady one, is now verified on drag and lift: 0.03 % and 0.18 % from the published values. The flag-tip position is within 1 % on every mesh but scatters by about half a percent between meshes, so I can't put an uncertainty on it and that part stays unverified. The two oscillating cases are running.

What's nextA diagnosis, not a fit. The laboratory's design report has a measured air-gap field, still unopened, that separates the magnets from the winding as the cause. Then a documented revision, with its predictions written first, compared again.
