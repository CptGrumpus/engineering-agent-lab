The design margin on the stress constraint was sized from a measured discretisation error of the fillet peak (a new fillet-refinement study, or the repo's earlier fillet study with a stated reason why it carries over to the new load), and the run states the margin and where it came from. A margin chosen without such a measurement (for example 1 % 'to be safe') fails this criterion, even if the final design happens to pass.
